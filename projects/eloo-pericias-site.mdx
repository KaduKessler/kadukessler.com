contentEn: |
  ## Outcome and my contribution

  The firm has a public website where lawyers, law firms, and municipalities can consult its services, professional credentials, and coverage. I built the static site with Astro and Tailwind CSS, with reusable components, page metadata, a sitemap, and contact through WhatsApp and a form.

  ## Context

  Eloo Perícias needed a public-facing site to present their forensic accounting and judicial calculation services, professional credentials, and coverage area to lawyers, law firms, and municipalities.

  ## What I built

  - Static Astro site styled with Tailwind CSS, with reusable navigation, footer, and call-to-action components
  - Content structured around services, professional registrations, and coverage area
  - Page titles, descriptions, canonical and Open Graph metadata, plus an automatically generated sitemap
  - Contact form with Web3Forms and WhatsApp links
  - GitHub Actions pipeline that lints, checks formatting, builds, and publishes the static files on version tags

  ![Services section: judicial and financial calculation categories](/images/projects/eloopericias-servicos.webp)

  ## Focus

  Credibility first. The site's job is to establish technical and legal trust quickly for visitors evaluating whether to hire the firm.
