contentEn: |
  ## Context

  Built for the Entrepreneurship and Solution Modeling course: validate a product idea end to end, from business model to a working MVP. Independent professionals often price services by gut feeling — this tool grounds the number in an official reference table instead.

  ## What I built

  - Fee suggestion based on the OAB/RS reference table, adjusted by service complexity and risk factors
  - Cash vs. installment comparison, with an alert and required justification when the effective value falls below the OAB reference minimum
  - Full calculation breakdown (base value, adjustments, discount, installments) for transparency
  - Persistent calculation history, with editing and clearing

  ## Focus

  Ground pricing decisions in an official reference instead of guesswork, in a flow fast enough for quick use on mobile.

  ## Note

  Repository and demo are currently private/offline while the deployment is revisited.

  ## Foco

  Ancorar a decisão de preço numa referência oficial em vez de chute, num fluxo rápido o suficiente pro uso no celular.
