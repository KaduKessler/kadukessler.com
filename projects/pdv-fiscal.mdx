contentEn: |
  ## Context

  A restaurant needed to start issuing fiscal notes to comply with a legal deadline. The point-of-sale system already existed, built by someone else; I fixed existing issues in it and integrated fiscal note emission on top.

  ## What I built

  - Full order-to-fiscal flow (table, counter, and delivery) through payment and NFC-e emission via FocusNFe, validated end to end in SEFAZ's test environment
  - Fiscal classification (NCM, CFOP, CSOSN, origin) reviewed and approved by the client's accountant, not guessed
  - Real DANFE display for the customer after payment, with access key and QR code
  - NFC-e cancellation flow respecting the legal 30-minute window, with its own panel and pending-status tracking
  - Diagnosed and fixed a broken account-creation trigger with no impact on real users

  ## Focus

  Getting the fiscal side right on inherited code and a real legal deadline, with the accountant's sign-off standing in for guesswork.

  ## Note

  In active development. Repository is private and belongs to the client.

  - Diagnóstico e correção de um trigger de criação de conta quebrado, sem impacto em usuário real

  ## Foco

  Acertar a parte fiscal em cima de código herdado e prazo legal real, com aprovação do contador no lugar de chute.
