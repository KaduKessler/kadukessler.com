contentEn: |
  ## From queue to export

  - **Queue.** Drag in multiple audio files, reorder them, and track progress and estimated time. You can cancel the current file or stop the whole queue.
  - **Review.** Click a timestamp to hear the corresponding passage. The editor highlights low-confidence passages so you know where to listen more closely, and supports find and replace. A separate history lets you find previous transcripts.
  - **Export.** Save the transcript as TXT, SRT, or WebVTT, or generate a PDF report with recording duration, recognition settings, and SHA-256 hashes.

  ## How I built Transcritor

  I built the Windows interface with Python and PySide6. PyAV decodes formats such as OPUS, OGG, MP3, M4A, WAV, and AAC. The app uses faster-whisper to run a selectable Whisper model locally on the CPU.

  I used SQLite to store the queue and history. Pending, interrupted, and failed files reappear when the app opens, and interrupted or failed files can be retried. The app writes the final TXT only when transcription finishes.

  Model downloads can resume after an interruption. The app checks available disk space and verifies the downloaded file's size and hash before replacing the installed model. The release build checks that the executable packaged with PyInstaller can download a Whisper model and transcribe sample audio.

  ## Use in a research project

  I later used Transcritor to process audio files from 31 Instagram Reels for an undergraduate thesis. The app generated 31 TXT transcripts, one per audio file, for consultation during the research.

  ## Da fila à exportação

  - **Fila.** Arraste vários áudios para o aplicativo, ajuste a ordem e acompanhe o progresso e a estimativa de tempo. Você pode cancelar o arquivo atual ou interromper a fila inteira.
  - **Revisão.** Clique em uma marcação de tempo para ouvir o trecho correspondente. O editor destaca trechos de baixa confiança para orientar a conferência e permite localizar e substituir texto. Um histórico separado permite encontrar transcrições anteriores.
  - **Exportação.** Salve o texto em TXT, SRT ou WebVTT, ou gere um relatório em PDF com a duração do áudio, as configurações usadas no reconhecimento e os hashes SHA-256.
